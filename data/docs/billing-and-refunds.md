# Billing and Refunds

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Enterprise customers can also pay by bank transfer against an invoice with 30-day payment terms. Payments are processed by Stripe; TidyShift never stores your full card number.

## Invoices

An invoice is emailed to the billing contact after every payment. You can also download all past invoices as PDF from **Settings › Billing › Invoices**. To add your company VAT number to future invoices, enter it under **Settings › Billing › Billing details**. VAT numbers cannot be added to invoices that were already issued.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry the card automatically 3 times over 7 days and email the billing contact after each attempt. If all retries fail, the workspace switches to read-only mode: managers can view schedules but cannot publish new ones. Updating the card under **Settings › Billing** restores full access immediately and charges the outstanding amount.

Workspaces that stay unpaid for 30 days are downgraded to the Free plan. No data is deleted.

## Refunds

- **Monthly plans:** we do not refund partial months. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the paid period.
- **Annual plans:** you can request a full refund within 30 days of the initial purchase or the renewal date. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, but you keep access until the end of the term.
- **Billing mistakes:** if you were charged in error (for example a duplicate charge), contact billing@tidyshift.example and we will refund the full amount, usually within 5 business days.

To request a refund, email billing@tidyshift.example from the billing contact's address and include your workspace name.

## Cancelling your subscription

Go to **Settings › Billing › Cancel subscription**. Only the workspace owner can cancel. After cancellation, the workspace moves to the Free plan at the end of the billing period. If you have more than 10 employees, the workspace becomes read-only until you remove employees or upgrade again.
