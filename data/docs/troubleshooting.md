# Troubleshooting

## Employees are not receiving notifications

1. Check that the schedule was **published**. Drafts never send notifications.
2. Ask the employee to open the mobile app, go to **Profile › Notifications** and confirm that push notifications are on.
3. On iPhone, check **Settings › Notifications › TidyShift** in iOS. On Android, check that battery optimization is not blocking TidyShift.
4. If the employee uses Slack notifications, confirm their TidyShift email matches their Slack email.

## The time clock says "You are not at this location"

GPS check-in requires the employee to be within the location's radius, which is 150 meters by default. Admins can change the radius under **Settings › Locations › Edit › Time clock radius** (between 50 and 1,000 meters). Make sure the employee has allowed location access for the TidyShift app. Indoors, GPS can be less accurate; a manager can approve a manual clock-in from the timesheet.

## Shift times are wrong by one hour

This usually means the workspace time zone or the employee's phone time zone is set incorrectly. Shifts are always shown in the workspace time zone set under **Settings › Workspace › Time zone**. Check that this matches the location.

## The invite link does not work

Invite links expire after 7 days. Open the employee's profile under **Team** and click **Resend invite**. If the employee already has a TidyShift account with another workspace, they should sign in first and then open the link.
