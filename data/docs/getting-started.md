# Getting Started with TidyShift

TidyShift is a shift scheduling app for small businesses such as cafés, retail stores and clinics. Managers build weekly schedules, employees see their shifts on their phone, and everyone gets notified when something changes.

> TidyShift is a fictional product created for this demo.

## Create your workspace

1. Sign up at app.tidyshift.example with your work email.
2. Enter your business name and time zone. The time zone is used for every schedule and reminder, so pick the one where your team works.
3. Choose a plan. Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is required for the trial.

## Add locations and roles

A **location** is a physical place where shifts happen (for example "Main Street Café"). A **role** is a job type such as Barista, Cashier or Nurse. Each shift belongs to one location and one role.

To add a location, go to **Settings › Locations › Add location**. To add a role, go to **Settings › Roles › Add role** and pick a color; the color is shown on the schedule.

## Invite your team

Go to **Team › Invite** and enter email addresses or phone numbers, one per line. Invited employees receive a link that is valid for 7 days. If the link expires, open the employee's profile and click **Resend invite**.

Employees can use the TidyShift mobile app (iOS and Android) or the web app. They do not need a paid seat until they accept the invite.

## Publish your first schedule

1. Open **Schedule** and select the week.
2. Click an empty cell to create a shift, or drag an existing shift to copy it.
3. When the week looks right, click **Publish**. Employees are notified by push notification and email.

Unpublished changes are only visible to managers. You can republish as often as you like; employees are only notified about shifts that changed.
