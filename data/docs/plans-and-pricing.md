# Plans and Pricing

TidyShift is billed per active employee per month. An active employee is anyone who was assigned at least one shift during the billing period. Managers who are never scheduled are free.

## Plans

| Plan       | Monthly price                         | Best for                                     |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Free       | $0, up to 10 employees, 1 location    | Trying TidyShift with a small team           |
| Starter    | $4 per active employee                | Single-location businesses                   |
| Pro        | $7 per active employee                | Multiple locations, payroll and integrations |
| Enterprise | Custom pricing, minimum 200 employees | SSO, audit log, dedicated support            |

### What each plan includes

- **Free:** scheduling, mobile app, shift reminders.
- **Starter:** everything in Free, plus shift swaps, time-off requests and unlimited employees on one location.
- **Pro:** everything in Starter, plus unlimited locations, time clock with GPS check-in, payroll export, and the Slack and Google Calendar integrations.
- **Enterprise:** everything in Pro, plus single sign-on (SAML), an audit log, a 99.9% uptime SLA and a named account manager.

## Annual billing

Paying annually gives a 20% discount on Starter and Pro. Annual plans are billed upfront for 12 months based on the number of employees at the time of purchase. If you add employees during the year, the extra employees are billed monthly at the discounted annual rate.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade at any time from **Settings › Billing › Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period.

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Features that are not part of the new plan (for example extra locations) become read-only, and no data is deleted.

## Nonprofit discount

Registered nonprofits get 50% off Starter and Pro. Email nonprofit@tidyshift.example with proof of nonprofit status to apply.
